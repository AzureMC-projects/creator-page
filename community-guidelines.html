Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Build with respect

Be constructive, honest and welcoming. Critique ideas without attacking people.

Keep people safe

No harassment, threats, hate, bullying, doxxing, impersonation, scams or deliberate disruption.

Build responsibly

Do not use Solance to create malware, steal credentials, evade security controls, facilitate illegal activity or infringe other people's rights.

Protect private information

Never post passwords, private keys, payment credentials, home addresses or other sensitive personal information.

Moderation

The Solance team may restrict or remove accounts and content that create safety or community risks. Serious issues may result in a ban.
