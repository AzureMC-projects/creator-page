Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Essential storage

Solance uses essential browser storage to remember consent choices and support core site and account functionality. These technologies are not used for advertising.

Optional preferences

If you choose to allow optional preferences, Solance may use browser storage to remember choices such as theme and reduced motion.

Third-party services

The site loads Supabase for authentication, database-backed applications and live status features. The site also loads the Supabase JavaScript client from jsDelivr. Third-party providers may use their own technical storage or logs as described in their policies.

Your controls

You can refuse optional preferences from the cookie banner. Your browser can also delete stored data at any time.

Changes

We will update this notice if the technologies used by Solance change.
